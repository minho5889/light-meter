## Verdict: APPROVED
- Reviewed by: Claude (reviewing architect)
- Independently verified a CLEAN build: zero warnings, 161 tests pass. Thank you for switching to `clean test` per the updated contract — this one was genuinely clean, nothing for me to fix.
- Dynamic Type is comprehensive: `@ScaledMetric` on all three primary numeric readouts (lux card, temperature, records row), every hardcoded `.system(size:)` replaced with a semantic text style (`.footnote`, `.caption2`, etc.), and the plan's specific complaint — the **9pt tab labels** — is now `.caption2`. `DesignConstants` gained a scalable `Font` palette.
- VoiceOver is well done: localized labels/values/traits on controls and readings, `.isSelected` on tabs and segmented controls, `.accessibilityElement(children: .combine)` grouping on the lux card / flicker gauge / records rows, and the real-time oscilloscope correctly marked `.accessibilityHidden(true)` (a waveform is noise to VoiceOver). Capture posts a localized `UIAccessibility.announcement`, and I verified the `String(format:)` specifiers match their arg types (`%d` Int / `%.1f` Double) — no garbled announcements.

- Non-blocking nits (candidates for R4 polish; not gating):
  1. A few VoiceOver-only strings are hardcoded English while the rest are localized — the equivalent-exposure cell ("Aperture f/X, shutter speed Y") and the flicker safety card ("Safety status: …", "Ready to check safety"). The records row and capture announcement, by contrast, are fully localized. Tidy for consistency (you already added `ui_aperture`/`ui_shutter_speed` keys in R1).
  2. `TemperatureCardView` got Dynamic Type but no explicit combined `accessibilityLabel` for the Kelvin reading — VoiceOver reads "3500" then "K" as separate elements. Serviceable, but less polished than the lux card's combined label. Consider a "<value> Kelvin, <tone>" label.
  3. Acceptance criterion "nothing clips at the largest Dynamic Type size" can't be verified without a device — the implementation is sound (scalable fonts + `.fixedSize(_:vertical:)` + `lineLimit`), but worth a real-device spot check at AX5. I'll flag this when you test on the iPhone 13 mini.

State: APPROVED
