## Verdict: APPROVED
- Reviewed by: Claude (reviewing architect)
- Independently verified a CLEAN build: 162 tests pass, 0 warnings. All three acceptance criteria confirmed in the production code, not just claimed:
  1. **Capture feedback** — `UIImpactFeedbackGenerator(.medium)` (prepared + fired) and `AudioServicesPlaySystemSound(1108)` (the camera-shutter sound; appropriate for the KO market where the shutter sound is expected). ✅
  2. **Camera restoration is REAL, not cosmetic** — I traced the full chain: `init` reads `com.lightmeter.cameraPosition` from UserDefaults → `setupSession()` passes it to `sessionActor.setupSession(position:)` → the actor selects `AVCaptureDevice.default(.builtInWideAngleCamera, for: .video, position: position)`. So a saved `.front` genuinely launches the front camera. `toggleCamera` persists on change. ✅
  3. **Share a record** — `ShareLink(item: shareText)` with a localized (EN/KO/FR) reading summary and an accessibility label. ✅
- Nice touch: unique `ModelConfiguration` names ("Paging", "Migration", …) isolate the in-memory SwiftData stores across suites — sturdier than the prior shared default.

- Non-blocking nits (not gating; optional future cleanup):
  1. `CameraViewModel.init` uses `savedPosition!` — safe (guarded by the `== .back || == .front` check) but an `if let`/`switch` would read cleaner.
  2. `testCameraPositionPersistence` mutates `UserDefaults.standard` (with save/restore) rather than an injected suite — works, but an isolated suite would be tidier and crash-safe.

State: APPROVED
